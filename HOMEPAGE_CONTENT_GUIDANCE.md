## 🔄 How It Works

### Step 1: **Setup Your Warehouses**
1. Add your warehouse locations (Gurgaon, Bangalore, Mumbai, etc.)
2. Configure store details and addresses
3. Set up user roles and permissions
4. Enable two-factor authentication

**Time Required**: 15 minutes

---

### Step 2: **Import Your Products**
1. Download CSV template
2. Add product details (name, SKU, barcode, variants)
3. Upload bulk products or add individually
4. Add product images and descriptions
5. Organize into categories

**Time Required**: 30 minutes for 100+ products

---

### Step 3: **Add Opening Stock**
1. Enter current stock levels for each warehouse
2. System creates inventory ledger entries
3. Set minimum stock levels for alerts
4. Configure reorder points

**Time Required**: 1 hour for initial setup

---

### Step 4: **Start Managing Operations**

**Daily Operations:**
- Receive new stock (purchases)
- Process customer orders
- Transfer stock between locations
- Handle returns and damages
- Monitor stock levels

**Automated Actions:**
- Low stock alerts
- Transfer suggestions
- Order notifications
- Stock synchronization

---

### Step 5: **Fulfill Orders**
1. Customer places order (online or manual entry)
2. System checks stock availability
3. Creates Shiprocket shipping order
4. Generates shipping label
5. Updates inventory automatically
6. Customer receives tracking info

**Time Required**: 2 minutes per order (automated)

---

### Step 6: **Monitor & Optimize**
1. View real-time dashboard
2. Check KPIs and metrics
3. Analyze movement patterns
4. Identify slow-moving products
5. Optimize stock distribution
6. Generate reports

**Ongoing**: Real-time insights
